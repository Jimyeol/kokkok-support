Privacy Policy

KokKok is a personal subscription tracker that stores the subscription information you enter on your own device.

Controller of personal information: mazecube

Contact email: crewoongcrewoong@gmail.com

Effective 2026-10-23 (previous version 2026-10-22)

No sign-in No ads No advertising ID collected Firebase usage stats & crash logs (excluding subscription details) No server backup Frankfurter exchange rate API

1. Information We Handle

KokKok stores the information you enter in the app on your device. This may include:

  • Subscription service name, category, and icon settings
  • Price, currency, billing cycle, first billing date, and planned cancellation date
  • Payment method notes, reminder settings, and notes you enter
  • App settings such as display mode, app language, region, base currency, and exchange rate settings

If you choose a photo as a subscription icon, the app copies the selected photo into the app's private storage. The app does not delete the original photo in your photo library.

2. About Estimates

The amounts, billing dates, countdowns, and statistics shown in the app are estimates calculated from the values you enter. KokKok does not check whether a payment actually succeeded, and it does not automatically cancel or change any external subscription service.

3. Data Sent Outside the App

KokKok does not send the subscription names, amounts, billing dates, payment methods, notes, or photos you enter to an app server.

If automatic exchange rate updates are turned on, the app sends a request for US dollar (USD)-based exchange rates to the Frankfurter v1 public exchange rate API. The request contains only the currency codes to look up (KRW, JPY, GBP, AUD, CAD, EUR) and does not include any subscription information or amounts you have entered. Frankfurter states that its public API does not collect or log personal data, IP addresses, or request URLs.

Frankfurter aggregates public reference rates from central banks and official institutions, and its v1 latest rates are updated daily based on the most recent business day. ECB statistics may be reused for commercial and non-commercial purposes, provided the source is acknowledged and the statistics are not modified. KokKok uses exchange rates only to calculate estimated amounts by converting the amounts you enter into the base currency you choose.

Exchange rate sources: Frankfurter v1 API, Frankfurter License & Terms, ECB statistics reuse policy. Source: Frankfurter and ECB statistics.

App Usage Statistics and Crash Logs (Firebase)

To improve the app, we use Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. The following information is sent:

  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): records of feature use such as screen views, adding, editing, or deleting subscriptions, adding widgets, and backups; the type of well-known service (e.g., netflix), category, billing cycle, and currency; the range of the number of subscriptions you have added; app version, device type, OS version, approximate country/region; and a random identifier assigned per app installation
  • Crash logs (Firebase Crashlytics): when the app crashes or encounters an error, the error details, device model, OS version, app version, and a random identifier assigned per app installation

Subscription names, amounts, billing dates, payment methods, notes, and photos are not sent. We do not collect advertising IDs (Android Advertising ID, iOS IDFA) or the iOS identifier for vendors (IDFV), and we do not use this information for advertising or personalized ads. This information is processed by Google LLC to provide the Firebase services and may be stored on Google servers outside your country (such as in the United States). It is deleted after Firebase's default retention periods (up to 14 months for usage statistics and 90 days for crash logs). No data is collected in debug (development) builds.

Service Logos

To show a service's actual logo in your subscription list, the first time a logo is displayed the app requests the icon from Google's favicon service or the service's own website using the service's address (e.g., netflix.com), and saves the received logo in the device's cache folder. This request does not include information you have entered, such as subscription amounts or notes.

Backup Files

"Export backup" in Settings creates a file on your device containing your subscription information, settings, and the icon photos copied into the app, and sends it to a destination you choose (a messaging app, cloud drive, the Files app, etc.). This file is not sent to the KokKok developer and is kept according to the policies of wherever you save it. Because the backup file contains your subscription names, amounts, and notes as entered, please store it somewhere only you can access.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications: used to send local notifications before the billing dates you set.
  • Photo library: used only when you choose a photo to use as a subscription icon.
  • Internet: used for automatic exchange rate updates, loading service logos, and sending usage statistics and crash logs.
  • Receive boot completed (Android): used to keep scheduled local notifications after the device restarts.

5. Retention and Deletion

Your subscription information and settings are stored on your device. You can delete individual subscriptions in the app or delete all subscription data in Settings.

When you delete all subscription data in the app, the subscription information stored by the app and the icon files copied into the app's private storage are also deleted. The original photos in your photo library are not deleted.

When you delete the app, data in the app's private storage is deleted according to the operating system's policies. However, if you have turned on iCloud, Google backup, device backup, or restore features, app data may be backed up or restored to a new device depending on your operating system settings.

To display home screen and lock screen widgets, upcoming payment information (service name, amount, billing date, and icon) is stored in the widget shared storage on your device. This information is not sent off your device.

6. Sharing with Third Parties and Outsourced Processing

We do not provide or share the subscription information you enter with third parties. We entrust the processing of app usage statistics and crash logs to Google LLC (Firebase) to improve the service. Exchange rate requests contain no subscription information and are used only to look up exchange rates.

7. Children's Privacy

KokKok is not a service directed primarily at children, and the current version does not offer sign-up or account features.

8. Changes

If sign-in, server backup, advertising, or payment features are added, or if the information we collect changes, we will update both this Privacy Policy and the app store privacy disclosures.

Changes on 2026-10-22: Added information about app usage statistics and crash logs (Firebase), loading service logos, exporting backup files, and home screen widgets.

Changes on 2026-10-23: Added that app language (Korean, English, Japanese), region, and base currency settings are stored on your device, and that exchange rates are looked up for multiple currencies.
